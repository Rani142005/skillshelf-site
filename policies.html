Policies & site information

This is starter text, not legal advice. Review and adapt it to your actual business, location, products and applicable laws before publishing.

Privacy policy

Explain what information you collect, why you collect it, which service providers process it, how long you retain it, and how visitors can contact you about their data. Do not claim to collect no data until you have checked your hosting, analytics, checkout and email tools.

Terms of use

Describe acceptable use of this website, intellectual property ownership, permitted use of downloads, limitations and contact details. Only publish resources you created or have permission to distribute.

Digital products and refunds

Clearly describe file formats, compatibility, delivery, support and your refund/cancellation policy. Make sure your policy complies with applicable consumer law and the terms of your payment provider.

Affiliate disclosure

If you use affiliate links, clearly disclose the relationship near relevant recommendations. Explain that you may earn a commission from qualifying purchases.

Contact

Add a monitored business email address before launch. Do not leave fictional or unmonitored contact details on a live site.
